# Story bank

Real examples, written once, used for "tell us about a time..." questions.
Keep each one true and specific: situation, what you did, what happened.

## Speeding up the nightly pipeline (Example Corp, 2026)
The nightly ingestion job took 3 hours and often missed the morning report.
I profiled it, found ingestion was serial, and parallelized it. It now runs
in 45 minutes.

## Document classification research (NYU Data Lab)
I fine-tuned small language models for document classification and designed
the evaluation, improving F1 by 12%.
